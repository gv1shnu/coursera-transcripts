In this unit, you learned how to insert and find documents in a collection with MongoDB. You also learned how to build your own queries with different comparison operators, such as gt, greater than, lt, less than, lte, less than or equal to, and gte, greater than or equal to. And logical operators, such as and and or. Operator give us more control over the results of our queries, which in turn makes our applications easier to develop.

Sometimes the right operator will make your query more specific, more accurate, and more performant. Finally, you learned how to query elements in an array and how to use the elemMatch operator. Congratulations on completing this unit. Now you can successfully insert and find documents in a collection.

As a next step, you can explore additional tasks, such as updating and deleting documents. This will allow you to perform all CRUD operations in MongoDB.
