Correct system design starts with knowing what not to do. Knowing what not to do is as simple as avoiding functional decomposition, but we're going to have to devote a whole hour for it simply because functional decomposition is the prevailing way of designing most systems and this is what you're likely doing right now. And so we're going to spend the first hour or so just discussing all the perils and the problems of functional decomposition and why it's precluded from actually ever working.
