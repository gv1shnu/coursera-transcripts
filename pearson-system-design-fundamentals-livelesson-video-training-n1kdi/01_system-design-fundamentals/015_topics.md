The next lesson is all about validation and composition. How do you know the design is any good? And how do you put together the components of the design into a coherent functioning system? Furthermore, how can such a design handle anything you can throw against it as far as future changes in the requirements?

We will discuss the very mission of all system designs and the essence of all good architecture.
