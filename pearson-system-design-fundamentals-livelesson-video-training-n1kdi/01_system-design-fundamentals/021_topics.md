The last lesson is all about additional design attributes you can associate with your design. It turns out that once you got the decomposition right, once you have the correct composition, there's additional aspects of the design, qualities of the managers and the engines, the behaviors across layers and interaction rules that you can put into play, which drastically increase the efficiency of your design. We'll discuss also what not to do, and we'll discuss the next steps, what comes up after system design.
